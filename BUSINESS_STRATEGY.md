## 1. Business Positioning

Welldone Metalworks is positioned as an **MS (mild steel) metal fabrication company serving Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar**.

The website must communicate a clear and consistent proposition:

> **Custom MS metal fabrication, designed, fabricated and installed for residential, commercial and practical outdoor applications in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar.**

The business should be presented as a fabrication specialist rather than a generic metal supplier.

### Core positioning

- Material specialization: **Mild Steel (MS) only**
- Service model: **Custom fabrication + site measurement + installation**
- Primary market: **Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar**
- Geographic focus: Ahmedabad-wide, including premium/posh residential and commercial areas, plus Gandhinagar
- Primary objective: Generate qualified enquiries through phone, WhatsApp, enquiry forms and quote requests
- Brand direction: Premium, dependable, practical, custom-built and locally accessible

# 2. Critical Business Rules

These rules are mandatory across the entire website, SEO metadata, structured data, advertisements, landing pages, blog content and AI-generated content.

## 2.1 Material restriction

Welldone Metalworks provides fabrication services using:

**Mild Steel (MS) only.**

The business does **NOT** provide:

- Stainless Steel (SS) fabrication
- Aluminum fabrication
- Stainless Steel gates
- Stainless Steel railings
- Aluminum pergolas
- Aluminum gazebos
- Aluminum sheds
- Any service that implies SS or aluminum fabrication

Do not use phrases such as:

- "MS, SS and aluminum fabrication"
- "metal fabrication in all materials"
- "stainless steel specialists"
- "aluminum fabrication experts"

If a visitor asks for SS or aluminum, the website should clearly communicate that those materials are outside the current service scope.

## 2.2 Installation and measurement

Welldone Metalworks **does provide site measurement and installation**.

This should be used as a genuine differentiator throughout appropriate service pages.

Preferred wording:

- Site measurement available
- Installation support provided
- Measurement and fabrication planning
- Fabrication followed by on-site installation where required

Do not claim 24/7 installation or emergency installation unless separately verified.

## 2.3 Testimonials

Existing testimonials are confirmed as **genuine** and may be used.

Do not invent:

- Customer names
- Company names
- Job titles
- Project values
- Locations
- Dates
- Ratings
- Additional reviews

Only use testimonials that exist in the repository or have been supplied/verified by the business.

## 2.4 Business hours

Do not claim that the fabrication workshop or installation team operates during the same hours unless verified.

# 4. Service Page Content Strategy

Every service page should be substantially different in content and search intent.

Do **not** create ten copies of the same template with only the service name changed.

## Target length

Each dedicated service page should generally contain approximately:

**900–1,500 words**

This is a content target, not a requirement to add filler.

Content should answer genuine customer questions and help a customer decide whether to enquire.

## Recommended service-page structure

### 1. Hero

- Service-specific H1
- Clear MS-only positioning
- Short benefit-oriented introduction
- Primary CTA: Request a Quote
- Secondary CTA: Call / WhatsApp

### 2. What We Fabricate

Explain what the service covers.

### 3. Where It Can Be Used

Discuss relevant applications without inventing unsupported use cases.

### 4. Customization Options

Explain what can reasonably be customized:

- Dimensions
- Layout
- Pattern
- Design
- Finish
- Installation requirements

Only mention options actually offered.

### 5. Our Process

A practical process such as:

1. Requirement discussion
2. Site measurement where required
3. Design/detail confirmation
4. Material and fabrication planning
5. MS fabrication
6. Finishing
7. Site installation
8. Final inspection/hand-over

Do not promise exact timelines unless verified.

### 6. Why MS

Explain the suitability of mild steel for the particular application without making exaggerated claims.

### 7. Installation

Clearly explain that site installation is available.

### 8. Service Areas

Mention Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar naturally.

### 9. FAQs

Use genuine, customer-focused questions.

### 10. Related Services

Link to relevant service pages.

### 11. Final CTA

Strong conversion section with:

- Phone
- WhatsApp
- Enquiry
- Quote request
